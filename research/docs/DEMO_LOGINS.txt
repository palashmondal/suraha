CITIZEN LOGIN  (no password — mobile + OTP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

  Go to    galachipa.suraha.net  →  লগইন
  Enter    any Bangladeshi mobile number, e.g. 01700000000
  The OTP appears ON SCREEN next to the message, like "(dev: 483210)".
  No real SMS is sent during the demo.

  Existing demo citizen:  01700000000
  Any new number creates a fresh citizen account on the spot.
